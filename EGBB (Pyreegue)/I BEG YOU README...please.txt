To Install this simply stupendous GSX Profile:
1. Move both .ini and .py files to %appdata%\Roaming\Virtuali\GSX\MSFS
Note: Even if you use 2024 and move the 2020 profile into your folder, it will only use the 2024 version (and vice versa). Umberto said so :D

2. Move the folder named 'hinshee-kt-gsx-bhx-textures' to your MSFS20/24 Community folder

It's that simple! Please Enjoy!
